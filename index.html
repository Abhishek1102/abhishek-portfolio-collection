About Me

Building mobile apps that actually matter

I'm Abhishek Panchmiya, a Senior App Developer based in Rajkot, Gujarat. I specialise in Flutter & Android (Kotlin) — building everything from the first line of code to the Play Store listing.

My work spans four domains: accessibility (Listening Eyes), dating (Hukup), e-commerce (Miodeal), and healthcare (Maia Care). I've worked solo and as part of cross-functional teams, owning features end-to-end.

I care about clean architecture, readable code, and shipping apps that are reliable in production — not just in demos. I also enjoy mentoring junior developers and collaborating directly with clients and backend engineers.
